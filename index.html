Nudge

Small Signals. Smarter Actions.

An AI guide that watches for small shifts in your sleep, focus and daily habits, measured against your own baseline. It does not diagnose.

  • A voice guide shown as a pulsing orb that speaks and explains
  • Cognitive tests built on published studies
  • Health device connections and a clinician portal
  • Demo mode so anyone can try it without an account

Chronos

Sales forecasting with an AI agent

Give Chronos your sales data. It cleans it, runs traditional, machine learning and deep learning models, compares them and forecasts with a range. Nora, the built-in agent, explains the results when you ask.

  • Excel and CSV input, one dataset or several merged
  • Models compared on 5 error measures, WAPE first
  • Fast, normal and deep runs with hyperparameter tuning
  • Live dashboard in Arabic and English with Egyptian holidays built in

WayMates

Ride matching across Egypt

Connects car owners and riders heading the same way. Matches are made on a shared part of the road, so trips do not need the same start and end points. The product ends at the introduction.

  • Recurring weekly trips and one-time trips
  • Arabic and English, maps on OpenStreetMap
  • Optional same-gender filter
  • WhatsApp or call contact once both sides accept
